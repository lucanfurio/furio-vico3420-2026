The Oldest Bar in Athens Ohio

Come have a meal and a drink at a bar full of history. The Pub is Athens, Ohio's longest standing bar. First opened up in 1974, The Pub has stood as a locally owned fixture of court street. We are known for our burgers, aquariums, and beer cheese soup, and our house shot is Sambuca.

And Great Burgers In Downtown Athens"
